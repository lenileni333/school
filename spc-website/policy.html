Policies

  1. We will make sure every student has a say, that everyone is comfortable in the things we change or add around the school and give students more opportunities to share new ideas that could be changed throughout the school.
  2. Our focus is on improving education, supporting student wellbeing and safety, making our canteen and facilities better, creating more opportunities for sport and recreation, and caring for our environment
  3. We will give students a voice through our website, listen to their ideas, and work to bring the most important and realistic suggestions forward.
  4. We believe leadership is not about making promises, it is about listening, working together, and taking action.
  5. We will get better toilets and make sure students can not vandalize the toilets.
  6. We will add more cameras around the school.
  7. We will have more EOTC days (education outside of the classroom) and hold special events so that people can socialize more.
  8. We will not tolerate any bullying/cyberbullying and we will take immediate action.
